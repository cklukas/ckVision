# WP-36A example coverage matrix

This matrix is the authoritative WP-36A mapping from public client surface to
example, executable evidence, visual/golden evidence, and documentation. WP-38
consumes this file for the final release-gate traceability pass.

Every widget's state × scheme goldens are in the
[appearance matrix](wp38-appearance-matrix.md) (`tests/golden/appearance/<Element>/`),
and every published screenshot is byte-checked by its capture tool's
`<tool>_visual_capture` test (D-112). The Golden column names the example-level
goldens and captures.

## Applications moved to CK Utilities

The TODO and desktop system-monitor examples left ckVision in 1.4.0 (D-151)
and continue as `ck-todo` and `ck-sysmon` in [CK Utilities](https://github.com/cklukas/ckUtilities), with their tests, goldens,
fuzzers and benchmark. Their capabilities are no longer claimed by this
table; [todo-example.md](todo-example.md) and
[sysmon-example.md](sysmon-example.md) remain as the delivery history.
