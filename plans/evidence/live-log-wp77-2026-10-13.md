IDs, revisions, pre-filter admission and eviction counters. LogModel supplies
per-record content revisions and explicit record attributes. LogView supplies
follow/pause/End-resume, unread counts, record/display-row navigation, wrapping,
horizontal inspection, mouse/keyboard selection and bounded grapheme-safe copy.
Unavailable anchors/selections are explicit. LogPanel composes SearchBox, Resume,
prioritized status and lifetime-guarded registry descriptors, with distinct
clear-view, clear-retention, stop and retry actions.

A single central row iterator now serves both text and grapheme wrapping without
a whole-line intermediate vector. The view preallocates viewport rows and one
record's wrap index. A counted provider test with 10000 retained entries proves
visible paint does not walk history. Separate allocation replacements prove
refresh/wrapping/scroll/selection/publication allocate nothing after construction.
Scrollbar proportional math uses 64-bit intermediates, exercised at a billion
records. Storage/capacity and horizontal/vertical track semantics are documented.
