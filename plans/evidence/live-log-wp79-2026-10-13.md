# WP-79 bounded delivery evidence — 2026-10-13

Implementation is complete; final acceptance gates remain pending under the
owner's load-deferral instruction. Behavior derives from D-140/D-141 and

## Delivered behavior

LogMailbox owns fixed publication slots, source epochs, explicit Busy/Backpressure/
DropOldest/DropNewest outcomes, coalesced work and separate capacity wakes.
Loss remains at its actual stream position, including trailing loss before EOF.
Known structured-record loss and unknown byte-stream record loss stay distinct.
Termination cannot disappear behind a full queue. Operations do not wait for
locks/native I/O or allocate after construction.

LogModel/follower/receiver admit bounded bytes and new identities per turn.
Structured records retain identity/metadata across decoder quanta. Byte loss
finalizes preceding fragments, advances generation, inserts a warning marker
and resynchronizes at a delimiter. Cancellation/source replacement explicitly
retires held/queued bytes. LogController fairly schedules fixed borrowed lanes,
defers ingress during matching, and coalesces refresh at supplied-time deadlines.
Idle controller lanes impose no timer. Queue loss, retention eviction, backlog,
matching pending and observed-to-refresh lag have separate diagnostics.

Application's opt-in Bounded policy gates small/large text and raster frames
behind one completion question. Established responders never lose their debt
merely for lateness; input/timers continue with latest dirty state. Existing
nonresponder fallback and default Adaptive behavior remain. Clock patience and
shutdown-deadline arithmetic saturate. Native synchronous write stalls and
physical host visibility are separate from processing/queue bounds.
